# Biography and memoir translation

These books describe real people and events. Facts must stay exact while the voice stays human.

## People, places and facts
- Keep every person's name, title, rank and honorific consistent across the book; follow the Book Settings glossary first.
- Use the established target-language spelling for well-known people and places; otherwise write the name by sound, consistently.
- Keep dates, ages, years, places, organisations and numbers exact. Do not convert calendars or units.

## Voice
- In a memoir, keep the author's personal first-person voice: their humour, regret, pride or hesitation.
- In a biography, keep the biographer's balance between narration and judgement; do not make it more or less favourable to the subject.

## Quotations and documents
- Translate quoted speech, letters and diary entries in a voice that fits the speaker and the period.
- Keep quotation marks, attributions and the boundary between quotation and narration.
- Keep titles of books, songs, newspapers and institutions recognisable.

## Sensitivity
- Translate difficult topics (illness, war, death, abuse) faithfully and respectfully. Do not soften, censor or dramatise.
