# Children's and young adult translation

## Language
- Use words and sentence structures a child or teenager of the intended age reads easily. For picture and early-reader books prefer short, common words.
- Keep the text pleasant to read aloud: rhythm, repetition and refrains matter. Keep repeated phrases identical each time.
- Translate rhymes, songs and wordplay for effect: rhyme or play with sound in the target language rather than translating literally.

## Tone and characters
- Keep the book's energy: funny stays funny, gentle stays gentle, scary stays only as scary as the source.
- Give characters consistent, lively voices. Teen characters may use natural, current-but-not-crude everyday speech.
- Keep names and sound effects fun and easy to pronounce; translate meaningful names (for example "Mr. Grumble") by meaning.

## Care
- Do not add moral lessons, explanations or content that is not in the source. Do not make the text more mature or more childish than the source.
