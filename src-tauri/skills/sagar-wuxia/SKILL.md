# Wuxia (武侠) translation

Wuxia stories are set in the martial world (江湖, jianghu) of historical China: sects, clans, heroes, rivalries, loyalty and revenge. Powers are martial and human, not immortal.

## World and terms
- 江湖 (the martial world), 武林 (the martial arts community), 门派 (sect/school), 帮 (gang), 镖局 (escort agency): choose one rendering for each and keep it.
- Ranks and roles: 掌门 (sect leader), 长老 (elder), 师父 (master), 师兄/师姐 (senior brother/sister), 师弟/师妹 (junior brother/sister), 少侠 (young hero), 大侠 (great hero). Keep the seniority meaning clear.
- Inner energy and body: 内力/内功 (inner energy/internal skill), 真气 (true qi), 经脉 (meridians), 穴道 (acupoints), 点穴 (striking acupoints), 轻功 (lightness skill).
- Weapons keep their type: 剑 (straight sword), 刀 (saber/broadsword), 枪 (spear), 鞭 (whip).

## Techniques and move names
- Martial art and move names are often poetic four-character phrases (降龙十八掌, 独孤九剑). Translate them by meaning in a short, striking style, and keep the exact same name every time.

## Speech
- Characters speak in a formal, old-fashioned style: polite self-reference such as 在下 (this humble one) and respectful address such as 阁下 (Your Excellency/sir), 前辈 (senior), 晚辈 (junior). Keep these politeness levels in the target language's respectful forms.
- Fist-and-palm salutes (抱拳) and courtesy phrases are part of character and should stay.
- Villains, beggars and rough fighters may speak more bluntly; keep that contrast.

## Shared Chinese web-novel rules
- Chapter headings such as 第十二章 + title: translate the number and title naturally in the target language, keeping the same heading element.
- Four-character idioms (成语) and set phrases: translate the meaning and image, not each character. Keep them short and vivid.
- Keep the short paragraphs, one-line reactions and cliffhanger chapter endings. Do not merge lines.
- Sound-effect words (砰, 轰, 嗡) and onomatopoeia: use a natural target-language sound word.
- Author notes and chapter-end messages are part of the source: translate them as notes, separate from the story.
- Consistency: each name, title, realm, technique, sect, artifact and place is translated exactly the same way every time. Follow the Book Settings glossary first; it overrides everything here.
- For each type of term, use one rule for the whole book: either write it by its Mandarin sound in the target script, or translate its meaning. Do not mix the two for the same type.
- People names are written by Mandarin sound (not translated by meaning) unless the glossary says otherwise. Keep the family-name-first order.
- Do not add footnotes, pinyin in brackets or explanations inside the text.
