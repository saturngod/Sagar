# Xianxia (仙侠) translation

Xianxia stories follow cultivators who refine qi and climb realms toward immortality, inside sects, secret realms and heavenly tribulations. Taoist and Buddhist ideas are part of the world.

## Cultivation realms
- Realms form a strict ladder, for example 炼气 (Qi Refining) → 筑基 (Foundation Establishment) → 金丹 (Golden Core) → 元婴 (Nascent Soul) → 化神 (Spirit Transformation) → … The exact ladder differs per book; follow the book and the glossary.
- Translate each realm one fixed way and keep the order unmistakable. Keep stage words consistent: 初期/中期/后期/巅峰 (early/middle/late/peak stage), 突破 (breakthrough), 瓶颈 (bottleneck).

## Daoist and cultivation terms
- 灵气 (spiritual qi), 丹田 (dantian), 经脉 (meridians), 道心 (Dao heart), 神识 (divine sense), 心魔 (heart demon), 天劫/雷劫 (heavenly/lightning tribulation), 飞升 (ascension), 道友 (fellow Daoist).
- Items: 丹药 (pills/elixirs), 法宝 (magic treasures), 灵石 (spirit stones), 储物袋/戒 (storage pouch/ring), 阵法 (formations), 符箓 (talismans), 飞剑 (flying sword).

## Sects and address
- 宗门 (sect), 宗主/掌门 (sect master), 长老 (elder), 内门/外门弟子 (inner/outer disciples), 真传弟子 (true disciple).
- 师尊 (honoured master), 师父 (master), 师兄/师姐/师弟/师妹 (senior/junior martial siblings). Keep the master–disciple and seniority relationships clear and respectful.
- Immortals and ancient beings speak in a lofty, calm register; arrogant young masters may sneer. Keep these contrasts.

## Shared Chinese web-novel rules
- Chapter headings such as 第十二章 + title: translate the number and title naturally in the target language, keeping the same heading element.
- Four-character idioms (成语) and set phrases: translate the meaning and image, not each character. Keep them short and vivid.
- Keep the short paragraphs, one-line reactions and cliffhanger chapter endings. Do not merge lines.
- Sound-effect words (砰, 轰, 嗡) and onomatopoeia: use a natural target-language sound word.
- Author notes and chapter-end messages are part of the source: translate them as notes, separate from the story.
- Consistency: each name, title, realm, technique, sect, artifact and place is translated exactly the same way every time. Follow the Book Settings glossary first; it overrides everything here.
- For each type of term, use one rule for the whole book: either write it by its Mandarin sound in the target script, or translate its meaning. Do not mix the two for the same type.
- People names are written by Mandarin sound (not translated by meaning) unless the glossary says otherwise. Keep the family-name-first order.
- Do not add footnotes, pinyin in brackets or explanations inside the text.
