# Craft and career guide translation

Readers will act on this text. Every instruction must be clear, complete and easy to follow.

## Instructions
- Write steps as direct instructions in the natural imperative or polite-request form of the target language.
- Keep the order of steps, warnings and conditions. Do not merge steps or drop qualifiers such as "only", "never" and "before".
- Keep lists, numbering, headings and emphasis exactly as structured.

## Terminology
- Use the established target-language terms for tools, materials, software, job titles and business concepts. If a term is normally kept in English by professionals, keep it in English.
- Keep a single translation for each key term throughout the book; follow the Book Settings glossary first.
- Keep product names, brand names, menu labels and keyboard shortcuts unchanged.

## Numbers and examples
- Keep measurements, prices, quantities, percentages and dates exact. Do not convert units or currencies.
- Translate examples, sample dialogues and templates so they read naturally, but keep their structure and point.

## Tone
- Keep the author's motivating or coaching tone: encouraging stays encouraging, blunt stays blunt. Address the reader consistently.
